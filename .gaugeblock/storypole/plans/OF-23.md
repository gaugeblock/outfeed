## 1. Objective
Overhaul the `outfeed release --all` orchestration engine to eliminate massive compilation waste, serialize LLVM bottlenecks, and excessive registry polling. Bring down full 10-repository releases from the empirical 147-minute baseline to under 3–5 minutes, and dry-run execution to under 10 seconds.

3. **Strict 250 LOC Law:** No file modified or added may exceed 250 lines of code.
